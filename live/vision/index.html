Some of the best ideas in Cyprus are still at school

We are building a platform for the young people who have them. A place to be heard, find support and change things, in Cyprus and around the world.

If there’s one thing we need right now, it’s new ideas.

While ideas usually begin with the individual, they grow best in conversation. Thinking needs its community. It needs disagreement, permission to fail. It needs space and lots of it, especially at a time when so many of those traditional spaces and the values underpinning them are hollowing out.

Our mission is to build a new space, a new community where this thinking can, hopefully, flourish: where difficult, controversial and deeply personal questions can be asked openly, collectively, from all angles, and most importantly, carried through into what actually gets built.

We want every young idea to get a fair chance to be

  1. heardA stage, and people who came to listen.
  2. noticedThe attention of people who can help.
  3. fundedMoney to try it for the first time.
  4. made realA way to act on it, and a change people can see.

Two lines we believe in

Ideas worth spreading

TED's line for about twenty years. It asks whether an idea is true, clear and useful enough to deserve someone's time.

Ideas change everything

TED's line today. It says what can happen next, when someone hears an idea and acts on it.

The first line teaches you to think. The second asks you to do something about it. Young people need both.

It starts with three skills

  1. Find an idea

    Notice what others walk past. Ask better questions. Turn a feeling that something is wrong into an idea of your own.

  2. Think it through

    Check the facts. Look for the best argument against you. Change your mind when you should.

  3. Make people care

    Tell it clearly and honestly, so that a room of strangers understands and wants to help.

What we will build

  1. An educational platform

    Where young people learn to find ideas, test them and present them.

  2. A national stage

    Events that bring the ideas of young people from across the island in front of the whole country.

  3. Cyprus branches of international competitions

    So that a student here can enter the same contests as students anywhere else.

  4. A road out into the world

    The best ideas go on to international events and competitions.

And a community around every idea

  • Businesses

    They bring real problems, real projects and people who know how things get made.

  • Government

    Public organisations show how decisions are taken, and where a young voice can change one.

  • Non-profits

    They know what their communities need, and what it takes to keep going.

  • Funds

    They help the strongest ideas take their first step.

Young people with ideas

While we build the rest, we will bring them together, from Cyprus and from around the world.

Where ideas meet the world

Real projects
Young people try themselves in work that matters to someone.
Real counterparts
They deal with professionals who ask hard questions and expect an answer.
Real stages
They speak to audiences who came to hear them.
Real places
They travel and visit the offices of companies, governments and non-profits, to see how an idea becomes a product, a law or a better life for someone.

Give young people the floor

They will live with the future the longest. We want them to help shape it now, starting in Cyprus.
