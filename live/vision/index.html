Good ideas are not only for adults

We want every student in Cyprus who has something to say to learn how to say it well, and to be heard.

Why we do this

Schools teach students to answer questions. A talk asks something harder: choose your own question, find out what is true, and make a room full of people care.

That takes research, honest writing, listening and courage. These skills last far longer than one evening on stage. A student who has built a talk knows how to think an idea through, take criticism and change their mind.

The programme uses TED-Ed's free Student Talks curriculum. We add coaching, a weekly group and a real audience.

What we promise

Curiosity comes first

We choose students for their questions, not for how confident they already sound.

Your idea stays yours

We help you sharpen it. No partner, teacher or parent decides what you say.

Facts are checked

Every talk stands on sources the student has found and can explain.

Always free

No fees for students or schools.
