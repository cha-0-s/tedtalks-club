From a first thought to a finished talk

Lessons 1 to 5

Discover

Learn what makes an idea, how to give and take feedback, and which of your ideas deserves a stage.

You leave with one clear idea.

Lessons 6 to 10

Develop

Research it, check your facts, find the story and write the script. Then edit it until every line earns its place.

You leave with a script you can defend.

Lessons 11 to 14

Share

Work on voice, body language and visuals. Rehearse with coaching, film yourself and see how far you have come.

You leave ready to speak.
