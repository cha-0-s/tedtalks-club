Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy covers this website, the list of people who asked us to email them about the launch, and applications to the programme. We collect as little as we can.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Artem Rodichev, organiser of the programme in Nicosia, Cyprus. Contact: arodichev@gmail.com. The Falcon School hosts the programme and does not receive the launch list.

Applications

If you apply, we collect your name, age, email, phone (if you add it), school, year, answers and video and, if you are under 18, the name, email and signature of your parent or guardian. We use them only to assess your application, contact you about it and, if you are selected, organise your participation. People make the decision, not software. The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR) and, if you are under 18, the consent of your parent or guardian as well.

Only the selection team sees your application. The team may include a coordinator from the host school. Your application video is never published, and we never share your data with sponsors or partners. If you are not selected, we delete your application and video within 3 months after the results. If you are selected, we delete them within 3 months after the final event. Tally removes deleted data from its backups within a further 90 days. The full notice is in the application form.

Launch emails: what we collect and why

  • Your email address, to email you about the launch of the programme and its applications. We first send you an email asking you to confirm the address.
  • Cookie and technical data, described below.
  • Your consent and the time you gave it, to show that you asked us to write to you.

The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR); for necessary cookies and security logs, it is our legitimate interest in running a secure website (Article 6(1)(f)). You do not have to sign up to be able to apply. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising or make automated decisions with it.

Students under 14

In Cyprus, a child can consent to an online service from the age of 14. If you are younger, please ask a parent or guardian to sign up with their own email address. If you think a younger child has signed up alone, tell us and we will delete the address.

Who processes your data

  • Tally (Tally BV, Belgium) provides the form and stores the answers on our behalf. Loading the form connects your browser to Tally, which receives technical data such as your IP address, and sets cookies. See Tally's GDPR page.
  • Make (Celonis, Inc., USA) passes your email address from the form to our mailing list. This may involve a transfer of data to the USA. See Make's privacy notice.
  • Mailchimp (Intuit, USA) stores the mailing list and sends our emails. It records whether you confirm or unsubscribe, and may record whether you open an email or click a link in it. This involves a transfer of data to the USA. See Intuit's privacy statement.
  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA) hosts this website and logs visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub's explanation. This may involve a transfer of data to the USA.

Only the programme's organisers can see the launch list.

Cookies

This website uses cookies, which are small files stored in your browser. Some are set by us and some by the services we use, such as Tally.

  • Necessary cookies make the website and the form work and keep them secure. They do not need your consent.
  • Optional cookies, such as those that measure how the website is used, are set only if you agree. You can change your mind at any time, as easily as you agreed.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The form may not work without the necessary ones.

How long we keep it

We delete your email address from the launch list when you withdraw your consent, and no later than 12 months after you sign up.

Your rights

You can withdraw your consent at any time, by using the unsubscribe link in any of our emails, replying to them or writing to the address above. This does not affect what we did before. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, to correct or delete it, to restrict its use, or to receive it in a portable format.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection in Cyprus.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its date.
